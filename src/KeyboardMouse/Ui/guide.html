Get going in 30 seconds

Caps

Turn it on

Tap Caps Lock. A small green dot appears next to the pointer — you are in mouse mode.

IJKL

Move

Hold a key and the pointer glides, getting faster the longer you hold. Press two for diagonals.

Space

Click

Space is the left button. Hold it while moving to drag. Tap Caps Lock again to type normally.

Quick reference

Power moves

Moving across a big screen with I J K L is fine — but these are much faster.

G

Grid jump

The screen splits into 9 boxes laid out like the keys under your right hand. Each press zooms into that box — any spot is 2–3 keys away.

  • U I O / J K L / M , . pick a box
  • Space click there · Esc cancel · Backspace go back
H

Hints

Every button and link gets a short label. Type the label and it is clicked — no aiming at all.

  • Labels use only home-row letters: A S D F J K L ;
  • Shift + label = hover · F + label = right click
D

Scroll layer

D+K

Hold D and the movement keys scroll instead of moving. Smooth, and it speeds up as you hold.

  • D + I/K up/down · D + J/L sideways
  • D + U/O page up/down · add Ctrl to zoom
B

Auto clicker

0

Press B and it clicks for you, again and again, wherever the pointer is. Keep moving while it runs. Press B again to stop.

  • The indicator turns yellow while it runs
  • Speed and button: Settings → Auto clicker

More moves

V

Drag lock

Holds the left button down so you can move freely. Press V or Space to drop.

T

Click & type

Clicks a text box and leaves mouse mode in one key, so you can start typing.

SA

Speed

Hold S for pixel-precise moves, hold A for turbo across the screen.

XCN

Jumps

Center of the screen, center of the window, or the next monitor.

M'

Marks

M then a letter saves the spot; ' then the letter jumps back. Saved across restarts.

CtrlAlt⌫

Panic

If a button ever feels stuck: releases everything and leaves mouse mode.

KeyboardMouse

v— · Free and open source (MIT)

Your privacy

  • Never records or saves what you type.
  • Stays offline unless you turn on update checks (GitHub only).
  • All source code is public on GitHub.

Good to know

  • Admin windows (e.g. Task Manager) only respond if KeyboardMouse runs as administrator.
  • Some games ignore simulated input.
  • Works together with a TrackPoint, touchpad or mouse.

Shutdown or restart

Shutdown stops the app completely and your keyboard goes back to normal right away. Restart closes it and starts it again.

Settings saved. Restart the app so every change is fully applied.
